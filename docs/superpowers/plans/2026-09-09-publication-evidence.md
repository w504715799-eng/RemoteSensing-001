## Required evidence before submission claims

- Contribution: answer a specific previously unresolved question; a weighted sum,
  variance identity, CRC reuse and more samples do not alone establish novelty.
- Baseline: source/version/formula audit complete, common input/support/risk/compute
  constraints, all adaptation differences disclosed. Published paper results cannot
  be directly compared numerically across different backbones/data/risk functions.
- Primary result: verify all final receipts and frozenmethods,8-method risk bounds,
  paired full-vs-w3 interval; retain risk failures, reject-all and unfavorable cases.
  Distinguish positive effect evidence from the prespecified5percentage-point target.
- Mechanism: beta0/beta1 and existing ablations provide controlled descriptive
  evidence; winning beta alone does not prove texture causality. Synthetic evidence
  establishes behavior/counterexamples, not prevalence or real accuracy improvement.
- Generality: current new method has one new data-domain study. Historical Spain
  experiments used different/older methods and are context only. Cross-domain or
  backbone generalization remains unproven without a separately specified evaluation.
- Automation: report risk-qualified coverage, paired gain, fixed32x32whole-tile
  pass rate and high-error acceptance. Workload counts are not measured human time.
- Compute: distinguish shared5seed research cost, isolated per-method deployment
  cost and two-process operational acceleration. Concurrent throughput is not a
  measurement of algorithmic speedup over a baseline.
- Reproducibility: source/protocol/code/runtime digests, fixed seeds, implementation
  differences, source/group assumptions, executable report and data availability.

## Analysis and additional-experiment discipline

Current primary claim and eight-method multiple-comparison allocation remain fixed.
Only read final test results after completion for the prespecified analysis. New
claims/subgroups/baselines/sensitivity tests must be independently specified and
labeled secondary/exploratory, with appropriate comparison control and honest data
reuse disclosure. Do not rename exploratory findings confirmatory or reset ledgers.

Current receipts retain scale samples, ROI curves/scalars and workloads, NOT full
feature/prediction maps. New spatial mechanism stratification or new scores cannot
be promised as CPU-only replay from those receipts. Inspect available artifacts
before declaring a supplemental experiment free of GPU cost. Do not reopen old
A/B/C or consumed terminal images/caches. A new independently evaluated method may
require an additional dataset/protocol and GPU computation; decide necessity from
scientific gaps, not from a desire to keep all GPU usage in this one run.

## Work after current GPU completes

1. Verify result integrity/role counts and produce all prespecified tables together.
2. Assess scientific go/no-go: effect/evidence/novelty, not p-value fishing. If effect
   is absent or evidence insufficient, narrow the conclusion or formulate a separate
   study; do not automatically rerun this one until favorable.
3. Implement any justified supplemental protocol and local tests before GPU use.
4. Select an appropriate publication scope/venue based on actual contribution and
   evidence; complete manuscript, reproducibility package, and independent review.
5. Request only genuinely missing author/affiliation/venue constraints when they
   become necessary for a concrete submission-ready artifact. No submission without
   explicit user authorization.
